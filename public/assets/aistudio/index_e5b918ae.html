ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Public information should be easy to access.

This student design-thinking prototype explores a more accessible way for citizens, businesses, journalists, researchers, civil-society organisations and Basotho living abroad to discover government news.

Prototype notice: the articles shown here are sample content for demonstrating the interface. They are not confirmed government announcements. A production website would need approved content, verified source links, publishing controls and secure data storage.
